# Warranty Policy

## Coverage period
Electronics, including headphones and speakers, have a 12 month limited warranty starting on the delivery date.

## What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal use. Battery capacity loss below 80 percent within 12 months is covered.

## What is not covered
Physical damage, water damage, unauthorised repairs and normal wear are not covered.

## Claims
A warranty claim needs the order number and a description of the fault. Approved claims are repaired or replaced, not refunded.
