# Order Cancellation Policy

## Before shipment
Orders with status CONFIRMED or PROCESSING can be cancelled free of charge. The payment is refunded to the original payment method.

## After shipment
Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled directly. The customer can refuse delivery or return the item after it arrives, or request a refund if the shipment is delayed or lost.

## Partially shipped orders
For orders shipped in several parcels, only the parcels that have not shipped can be cancelled.
