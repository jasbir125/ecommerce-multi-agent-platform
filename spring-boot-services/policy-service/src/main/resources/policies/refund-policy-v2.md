# Refund Policy

## Delayed shipments
Orders delayed by three (3) or more days beyond the promised delivery date are eligible for a full refund of the order amount. The customer may instead choose a cheaper replacement (a lower price than the original item).

## Lost shipments
A shipment is treated as lost when the carrier shows no scan for five (5) days. Shipments confirmed lost by the carrier are eligible for a full refund. A carrier exception case must be opened once per shipment.

## Refund amount
The refund can never exceed the amount paid for the order. Shipping fees are refunded together with the order amount when the delay or loss is the carrier's fault.

## Approval
Every refund must be approved by a manager before it is issued. Refunds above 500 USD require approval from a senior manager. Approvals expire after 30 minutes if nobody decides.

## Refund method and timing
Refunds are returned to the original payment method only. Customers can expect the money within 5 to 7 business days after the refund is initiated.

## Shipped orders
An order that has already shipped cannot be cancelled directly. The refund is processed after the carrier exception case is opened.
