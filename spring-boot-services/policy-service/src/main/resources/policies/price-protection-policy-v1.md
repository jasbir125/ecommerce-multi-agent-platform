# Price Protection and Replacement Policy

## Replacement price
When a replacement is offered for a delayed or lost order, the replacement should cost the same or less than the original item. A more expensive item may only be offered if the customer pays the difference.

## Stock
Replacements must be in stock. Stock is reserved before the replacement order is created, and the reservation is released if the replacement order fails.

## Similar products
A replacement should be in the same product category as the original item. If no similar item is available the customer is offered a refund only.

## Price drops
If the price of an item drops within 14 days after purchase, the customer may request the difference as a price adjustment.
