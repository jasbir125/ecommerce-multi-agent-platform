# Shipping Delay Policy

## When a shipment counts as delayed
A shipment is considered delayed when it has not arrived three (3) days after the promised delivery date. Delays caused by severe weather or customs inspections are announced on the tracking page and do not start the three day count.

## Lost shipments
When a shipment shows no carrier scan for five (5) days it is treated as lost. A carrier exception case must be opened once per shipment, and the customer is offered a refund or a replacement.

## What customers are offered
For a delayed or lost shipment the customer chooses between a full refund and a replacement. Express upgrades are not offered as compensation.

## Tracking updates
Tracking information is refreshed from the carrier every four hours. Support agents must verify tracking status with the carrier before promising a delivery date.
