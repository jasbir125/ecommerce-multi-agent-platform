# Returns Policy for the European Union

## Cooling-off period
Customers in the European Union may withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 14 days after the withdrawal is received, to the original payment method.

## Return costs
The customer pays the cost of returning the goods unless the item was defective.
