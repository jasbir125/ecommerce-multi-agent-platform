# Returns Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free when the item arrived damaged or defective. Otherwise a flat return fee of 6 USD is deducted from the refund.

## Non-returnable items
Opened software, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## Inspection
Refunds for returns are issued within 5 business days after the returned item passes inspection.
