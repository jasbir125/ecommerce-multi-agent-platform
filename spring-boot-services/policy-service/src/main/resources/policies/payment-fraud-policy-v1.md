# Payment and Fraud Review Policy

## Refund destination
Refunds are only ever sent to the original payment method. They are never sent to a different card, account or person.

## Fraud review
Accounts with more than three refunds in 90 days are reviewed by the fraud team before a new refund is approved. Refunds above 500 USD always get a manual risk review.

## Chargebacks
If a customer opens a chargeback with the bank, no separate refund is issued for the same order.

## Identity
Support agents must confirm that the person asking for a refund owns the order before any refund is requested.
