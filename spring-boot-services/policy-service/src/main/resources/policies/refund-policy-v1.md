# Refund Policy (legacy)

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 7 days of delivery for items that are unused and in original packaging.

## Delayed shipments
Delayed shipments are not covered by this legacy policy. Customers with delayed orders must wait for delivery and then follow the standard return process.

## Refund method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
