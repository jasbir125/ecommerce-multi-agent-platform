# Customer Communication and Support SLA

## Notifications
Customers are notified by email when a refund is initiated and when a replacement order is created. Messages state only confirmed outcomes.

## Escalations
Cases escalated to a human agent receive a first response within one business day.

## Phone support
Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 local time.
